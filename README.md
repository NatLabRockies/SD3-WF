# SD3-WF: Secure-by-Design DER Deployment — Workforce Tool

**SD3-WF** is an open-source, web-based training platform for students and professionals working at the intersection of cybersecurity and distributed energy resources (DERs). It provides an interactive, experiential environment for recognizing, diagnosing, and responding to cyber attacks on the cyber-physical systems that make up the modern grid edge.

## What is this tool?

Behind-the-meter technologies — residential and commercial systems like battery storage, smart thermostats, building automation systems (BAS), and electric vehicle (EV) chargers — are rapidly becoming integrated participants in grid operations. Aggregated through virtual power plants or demand response programs, these devices can be coordinated at scale to balance load, provide ancillary services, and support grid stability. But that connectivity cuts both ways: the same communication pathways that enable coordination also introduce cyber vulnerabilities that can propagate from a single compromised device to affect broader grid operations.

Designing and operating these systems securely requires cross-domain expertise spanning power systems engineering, building controls, communications protocols, and cybersecurity — a combination that is difficult to develop through traditional classroom instruction alone.

SD3-WF addresses this gap by connecting learners with novel, cross-domain datasets generated by its parent tool, [SD3 (Secure-by-Design DER Deployment)](https://github.com/NatLabRockies/sd3-tools), which uses system-of-systems emulation to quantify cyber risks across these interconnected systems. Where SD3 is designed for engineers and planners making real deployment decisions, SD3-WF is designed for **learning** — offering an exploratory environment that builds the cognitive skills needed to make sound cybersecurity decisions during both the design and operation phases of DER deployment.

## How it fits into the national CIE strategy

SD3-WF supports the **Education pillar** of the U.S. national [Cyber-Informed Engineering (CIE)] https://inldigitallibrary.inl.gov/sites/sti/sti/Sort_141694.pdf) strategy, developed in alignment with the CIE Curriculum Guide. The parent SD3 tool addresses the Development, Current Infrastructure, and Future Infrastructure pillars — together, they span the full CIE strategy from conception and design through workforce development.

## Who is this for?

- **Students** in energy systems, cybersecurity, or electrical engineering programs
- **Professionals** seeking hands-on familiarity with DER cyber-physical systems
- **Educators** looking for open curriculum to accompany grid-edge cybersecurity instruction
